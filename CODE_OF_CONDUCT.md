# Code of conduct

[English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | [简体中文](docs/zh-CN/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, patient, and specific. Critique ideas and code, give useful context, and respect different communication styles. Questions in any language are welcome.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, restrict participation, or ban repeat or severe offenders. Decisions should reflect the impact and context; maintainers are subject to the same standards.

Report public conduct problems with GitHub's **Report content** action or contact the repository owner through the contact options on [their profile](https://github.com/philpm). Share sensitive details only through a private channel. Maintainers will limit disclosure to people needed to handle the report. Appeals may be sent through the same private channel with additional context.

For software vulnerabilities, follow [Security](SECURITY.md).
